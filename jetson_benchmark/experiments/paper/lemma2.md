# Proposed revision to Section 5

This replacement concerns Protocols 1–3 in *Facerecognition-12.pdf*, §5, and the reference-template objection in review 259A. All products below use the protocol's fixed-point inputs and specified ring. The underlying real-valued face vectors are distinct from their encodings.

**Replacement for Lemma 2 (information disclosed by reconstruction).** In an honest execution of Protocol 3, a drone that observes both its local output shares and the wireless device's reply learns the encoded inner product $u=\langle x,y\rangle$ and the encoded squared reference norm $v=\langle x,x\rangle$. Both are deterministic functions of its view. Therefore any claim about the privacy of the complete drone view must account for these two values in addition to the verification decision.

**Proof.** By correctness of Protocol 2, summing the two parties' shares of each output in the ring gives $u$ and $v$, respectively. Protocol 3 supplies the missing shares to the drone. Consequently applying this public reconstruction to the drone view recovers $(u,v)$ with probability one. For a fixed probe, two enrolled inputs can yield the same decision and different reconstructed values. For example, use the encoded probe $y=(1,0,\ldots,0)$ and references $x_0=(2,1,0,\ldots,0)$ and $x_1=(1,2,0,\ldots,0)$. Both references have squared norm 5 and both pass either experimental threshold. Their reconstructed pairs are $(2,5)$ and $(1,5)$, respectively. Even conditioning on the decision and reference norm leaves distinguishable views. Thus the original equality of view distributions for arbitrary references in Definition 5.1 is false. ∎

**A scoped replacement for the original theorem.** For the *idealized arithmetic protocol* in the paper, fix the public parameters, an honestly generated verification, a passive non-colluding drone, a trusted dealer, and the drone's own input $y$. Assume independent uniform additive mask shares and independent uniform drone-side shares of the dealer's multiplication correlations. Given $y,u,v$, a simulator samples the drone's mask and correlation shares as in setup, samples the masked reference uniformly, samples the complementary live-mask share to form the full live mask and masked probe consistently, and computes the drone's local outputs by Protocol 2. Generate the WD's two reply shares as $u-o_{xy,0}$ and $v-o_{xx,0}$ in the ring. These replies are exactly the replies determined by correctness. Running the local decision algorithm gives the rest of the honest arithmetic state. The masked reference is uniform conditional on the drone's mask share because the WD's reference-mask share is fresh and uniform. This establishes equality of the ideal arithmetic views for references giving the same specified leakage and fixed drone input. Any implementation-level claim must additionally account for auxiliary keys, setup files, observed traffic, timing, retained state and the actual corruption model.

**Changes needed elsewhere in the manuscript.**

* Retain Lemma 1 for the masked reference and one mask share. Replace Definition 5.1 and Theorem 5.1 with the scoped leakage statement, and revise conclusions that cite unconditional reference confidentiality.
* For multiple runs, specify the leakage sequence $(u_k,v,b_k)_k$ for the observed probes. Fresh preprocessing re-randomizes the shares. It does not erase deterministic information already opened to the drone. Remove the extension to arbitrary active drone behavior absent a separate proof.
* Distinguish capture during execution from capture after effective erasure. Specify whether earlier transcripts were recorded. The baseline drone knows the live vector and full live mask during execution. Retaining the full mask together with recorded masked coordinates reveals that live vector. The original timing fixture even serializes the live vector and mask in the drone's role file (`obscura-protocol/dealer.py`). This fixture cannot substantiate a secure-erasure claim.
* For both revised TEE variants, the Linux process already holds the live embedding and supplies its fixed-point representation to the TA. Linux also sees the outgoing masked vector. Consequently Linux can compute the full one-use mask as the masked vector minus the known fixed-point probe, coordinate by coordinate in the ring. Isolated mask storage by itself gives no live-template or post-output mask secrecy against that host. In the masking-only variant, Linux still reconstructs the two reference-dependent scalars. In the reconstruction-in-TEE variant the TA opens those scalars and the host receives the decision. The return tag authenticates the WD shares. The TA takes the drone-side shares from Linux, so correctness against maliciously modified local shares requires an additional mechanism or an explicit trust assumption. A claim about the host's view requires a separate argument covering this variant's messages, supported number of queries, one-use provisioning, TA isolation, non-collusion and passive behavior. A one-board performance experiment places both roles on the same host.
